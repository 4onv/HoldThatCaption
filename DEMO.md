# HoldThatCaption · 3 minute 40 second demo

Record the browser with tab audio. Start in the coffee demo with **Original** selected. If a fresh viewer is available, capture their first attempt before they see the editor. Ask whether you may show their anonymous response. A second fresh viewer can try the edited version. When recording solo, call your own tap a walkthrough.

| Time | On screen | What to say or do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:25 | Play the original coffee short. Leave the pause intact. | “Watch the last line. When did you know the joke?” Let the viewer hear “grounded,” then point to where it first appeared in the caption. |
| 0:25–0:55 | Select the final cue on the timeline. Scrub between 4.70 and 7.10 seconds. | “The whole sentence appears at 4.70. The actor says the last word at 7.10. A viewer reading along gets the ending 2.40 seconds early.” |
| 0:55–1:30 | Select **grounded.** Show the marked reveal. Move it earlier by 0.1 seconds, then back. Apply the timing fix. | “I choose which word to hold. I can mark the reveal from the playhead or adjust it by a tenth of a second. The setup stays readable during the pause.” Let the edited passage play. |
| 1:30–2:00 | Open **Compare cuts** and play both passes. | Let the two passes run without talking. Point out the identical audio, the changed word timing, and the 1.60 seconds left to read the final word. |
| 2:00–2:50 | Show **Test with a viewer** or your recorded first attempt. | The viewer sees a muted clip. A tap pauses it and hides the frame. They type their guess before playback reveals anything else. Show which cut they watched, what they guessed, and when they tapped relative to the marked reveal. A no-tap run also produces a note. |
| 2:50–3:15 | Open **Viewer notes** and download the notes. | Describe what actually happened. An early wrong guess has a different interpretation from an early correct one. If the attempt is yours, use this time to explain what you would test with a fresh viewer. |
| 3:15–3:40 | Export SRT and open the downloaded file. | Show the original setup ending at 7.100 seconds and the reveal starting at the same instant. “I can take this back to my video editor as ordinary subtitles.” Credit the actual project contributions and tools you used. |

For a three-minute cut, shorten the timeline explanation and show one viewer note. For a longer version, import your own matched clip and captions and demonstrate a second edit. Save viewer notes and the SRT before replacing a clip or closing the tab.
